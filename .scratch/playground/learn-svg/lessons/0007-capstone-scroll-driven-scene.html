Capstone — the scroll-driven scene

Everything at once: three stacked boxes that rotate and explode apart as you scroll. Every technique from Lessons 1–6, assembled — this is a miniature of your prototype. Read the annotated source, then scroll the stage below.

The seven ideas, in one file

  1. L1 primitives — faces are <polygon>s.
  2. L2 pivot — centred viewBox so rotation is clean.
  3. L3 rotation — rot2() spins x,y before projecting.
  4. L4 projection — proj() is the iso P(x,y,z).
  5. L5 animation — a rAF loop + lerp smooth-follow on the scroll value.
  6. L6 painter's algo — faces sorted far→near, all drawn.
  7. the mission — a scroll-driven exploded axonometric, in ~60 lines.
